# Last Light — the characters you meet at the bar
Who they are, what happens in their stories, and every level. Spoilers for all of them.

## Frebby

*In the game.*

**Who she is:** a companion robot, F-series, who sits at your bar on Thursdays after her shift. When you meet her, her left arm is missing: there is a chrome cap and some loose cables. She is dry, flat and guarded, and she pays for everything herself, always too much, because to her every kindness has a price. She wasn't built: she was a four-year-old girl who was copied into a machine. She wants to buy her own arm back. What she needs is to let somebody be kind to her for free.

**What happens:** She comes in crying, lets you choose her drink, offers you her 212 factory "profiles" and then tells you she was once a person. She comes back after work with a rented arm that doesn't fit, refuses a free spin and won't talk about her past. Then her partner Lev leaves her for someone who can grow old, and she spends a rainy day on your floor; Selina won't let her pay, and for the first time she doesn't. On a good day she tells you everything: she died of a fever at four, a company bought her mind from her parents, and she was rented out from five, all legal, because a robot body has no age. You call your old friend Arda, who was copied at eleven. He brings Barnaby the bear for the shop, fixes her arm, and they leave together. In her last visit she has bought her own arm back, with the strawberry sticker she chose at nine still on the wrist, brings a wedding invitation from her and Arda, and lets you pay for her drink.

**Levels:**
1. Something That Burns: Selina is already talking to her and whispers that she was crying; she lets you choose her drink, offers the cheerful profile, says "I was a person", and says someone paid for her arm.
2. After Work: she has a rented arm; her own arm is in a pawn shop because she missed a payment; she refuses a free spin, laughs once at Selina's stolen coat and the NOOD noodle stand, and won't answer questions about before.
3. Rain: Lev has left her; she sits soaked on the floor, talks about "renters", and asks if anyone ever left you; she leaves without reaching for her coins.
4. Patterns: her whole story, in her own words; you call Arda, who brings Barnaby (customers stay a little longer) and fixes her arm for the price of noodles; they leave together.
5. Invitation: her own arm back, the wedding card, and "this time you're paying"; she remembers what you said in the first four levels, asks about Ada, and never comes back.
- Evening: Two Hundred and Twelve: she plays two of her factory profiles and asks what yours would be.
- Evening: Something Warm: she can't taste the noodles she buys, she only holds them because the box is warm; she asks what you ate as a kid.
- Evening: Four Hundred Dances: she knows four hundred dances and has never picked one; Selina pulls her off the stool.

## Potoron

*In the game.*

**Who he is:** a short, round man who lives inside his VR headset, with cables to a humming backpack and a tall antenna, and a voice like a kettle. He has no shame and no filter: he falls in love fast, panics fast and is fine again ten seconds later. He has no memory chip, and he says he is the best hacker alive: the Concordat wants the person who draws cartoon genitals on their chip billboards, and that person is him. Under the jokes there is one sincere thing: inside the headset, nobody looks at him.

**What happens:** You meet him moaning between your machines in the middle of a VR premiere; if you are too slow, he leaves a puddle on the floor and goes. He comes back to tell you it was the best porn of his life, shows off his rig and starts the director's cut before he is out of the chair. One Saturday he runs in sure the machines will burn everyone's chip, learns you have no chip either, and tells you who he really is. A broken update makes him fall for Last Light, and he asks you if anybody could love a face like his. Then a real Concordat drone comes for him, you hide him, and you see his face for the first time. In his last visit he puts an antenna above your sign as a gift, says he has quit porn (four hours ago), takes the headset off and spends an hour in your shop with his real face.

**Levels:**
1. Meeting: you catch him mid-premiere; nobody shakes his hand; he gives his name and, on the way out, waves at Last Light instead of you.
2. The Best One Ever: the best porn of his life, a cigarette, two Jackpots, the rig and the satellite that doesn't exist; "That sounds lonely" stops him for a second.
3. They'll Burn Us: he is sure the machines read and burn chips; you have none either, so he tells you he is the hacker; his mother spent his chip money on a bakery.
4. She Said Try Again: his romance filter is broken and he is in love with Last Light; she says "no win"; he asks if anybody could love his face.
5. The Drone: a Concordat drone comes for him and you cover for him; he asks if you ever had to hide; first time you see his face.
6. Best Signal in the District: an antenna above your sign, four hours without porn, one hour with his real face; he remembers what you said in levels 3, 4 and 5, and doesn't come back.
- Evening: Real Graphics: he is amazed by real light and real rain, and asks for the best thing you've ever seen with your own eyes.
- Evening: The Whistle: Selina asks about his voice; he does a kettle.
- Evening: Mum Calls: his mother calls in VR, and all he can see is her nose; you say hi.

## Keena

*In the game.*

**Who she is:** a professional mourner. Rich families pay her to cry at funerals, beautifully, on cue, and she carries a rate card: single tear, twenty; the full wail with knees, a hundred and fifty; weekends double. She rates everything out of ten and never gives tens. She hasn't cried for real in two years. Her real name is Mara, and the State's grief-call machine, also called Mara, was built out of her voice, her face and her crying.

**What happens:** She rehearses a wail at your bar and asks when you last cried. She fixes a stranger's crying in five lines, and tells you she learned it at seventeen as an AIX "reference", crying on cue in rooms full of sensors. When a customer gets the grief call in your shop, the voice is hers: AIX bought it, and her name with it. At the televised funeral of the year she can't cry, the family switches on Mara, and her career ends live. She comes in drunk and tells you that Mara called her the night her father died, and she hung up on herself. In her last visit, in a yellow coat, she tells you about Hüseyin's funeral on the walk, offers to cry for one of your dead for free, and finally cries for real, badly, for her father. She takes her name back and pins her taped-up rate card behind your bar with one new line: "Friends: free."

**Levels:**
1. Rehearsal: a woman is wailing at your bar, and it's a rehearsal; she hands you her rates and asks when you last cried, and meets someone whose tears were real.
2. Chin Work: she coaches a crying customer ("You're crying from the chin") and tells you about AIX's reference rooms; she asks if her perfect tear is any good, and you can tell her that's what's wrong with it.
3. Mara: a young man gets the grief call at your machines, and the voice is hers; she hangs up on her own voice and sits with him, or watches you or Selina do it.
4. The Funeral of the Year: on live TV she can't cry and Mara replaces her; drunk at your bar, she tells you about her father; you give her the Lamp Oil she asks for or a secret Night Shift, and in the morning her rate card lies torn in half on your couch.
5. Free of Charge: Hüseyin's funeral, then her first real cry in two years; she takes back the name Mara, starts sitting with people the machine calls, and leaves "Friends: free." behind the bar.
- Evening: Off Duty: she rates your evening crowd and asks what you want at your funeral.
- Evening: The Lesson: she tries to teach you to cry on cue ("think of one shoe in the road").
- Evening: Ash to Orbit: OpenSpace shoots ashes at the Ring, and she would rather have a knock on the door than Mara's call.

## Feli

*Not in the game yet.*

**Who she is:** a tall welder from Duisburg, in Germany, with goggles on her head, a tool belt and work boots. She speaks Denglisch: English with German words and German word order. Everyone on the walk expects a punctual German engineer with a system; she is always late, failed maths three times, and keeps her money in her boot, but she can fix almost anything by hand. She says sorry before anyone asks and writes next week's excuse in advance. Every night at ten she waves up your stairs at the Ring, because her mother in Germany is waving at it too.

**What happens:** She fixes Selina's screaming coffee machine with one slap. The walk bets on when she'll turn up and follows her made-up "system" at your machines until a woman loses her bus money; then Feli mends the motor in Ada's star mobile with one tiny spark. She turns your bar into a space station for her mother's video call, then admits she is not on the Ring: she missed the rocket by eleven minutes, and still pays for the seat while sending Mama "what a Ring welder sends home". When OpenSpace marks her "unreliable" for good, she takes Mama's money to Last Light and tells you why she was late: Mama's tearful goodbye call. In her last visit she comes four hours early, tells Mama the truth on your TV with the whole hall watching, and learns that Mama kept every payment in a tin marked RING. She stays as the walk's welder.

**Levels:**
1. Sorry, I'm Late: she runs in, fixes the coffee machine with a slap, asks two for it (you can pay twenty) and waves at the Ring at ten.
2. The German Engineer: the walk bets on her and follows her fake system until a woman loses her bus money; you tell the truth, let it run (+40) or send everyone home (−20); she mends Ada's star mobile.
3. Zero Gravity: your bar becomes a space station for Mama's call, and you can switch the machines off (−40) and play the robot; afterwards she tells you she is not on the Ring.
4. Eleven Minutes: OpenSpace drops her for good, and she takes Mama's money to Last Light; you stop her, let her lose it (+120 for you) or lend her eighty (−80); she tells you about the phone call.
5. On Time: four hours early, she tells Mama the truth on your TV; Mama says "Then it was the best phone call I ever made", Feli stays as the walk's welder, and your eighty comes back if you lent it (Rep +2).
- Evening: The Excuse Book: her best excuses of the year, judged by you and Selina; page one isn't in the competition.
- Evening: Ear Worm: German words for Selina (Ohrwurm, Treppenwitz, Fernweh, Heimweh), and whether you long for far places or for home.

## Ruta and Kostas

*Not in the game yet.*

**Who they are:** Ruta and Kostas have been married forty-six years, and they have never made a decision without flipping a coin: who talks, who orders, who pays. She was a tailor; he carried furniture for forty years. Now they are getting divorced, and they split their things at Last Light, one thing every Friday, with you as their witness. He is seventy-four, but he looks twenty-six: a second body from NeuroChip, on a payment plan. They finish each other's sentences.

**What happens:** They split a lamp, then their old dog Kofte, who growls at Kostas's new smell. Then their sofa gets stuck in your door, Ruta slips, Kostas carries her in, and the truth comes out: the lift in their tower has been broken for two years, her hip went the same month, and he bought the young body to carry her down thirty-one floors. For the flat, each of them secretly asks you to make the other one win. The machine stops on a moth, and whatever you read out, they have the first loud fight of their marriage in the middle of your hall. Underneath it, she wanted to live somewhere she could walk out of on her own, and never said so. In the last visit they argue out loud for fun, order their own drinks, cancel the divorce, move to a ground-floor flat by the canal and leave you the coin.

**Levels:**
1. Two Witnesses: they split a lamp at Last Light while the hall bets, and the coin decides even who talks; you get a witness fee (+10, Rep +1).
2. Kofte: the machine gives the dog to Kostas, the dog disagrees, and you decide if the dog may choose; we learn the coin started with a green sofa in their first year (Rep +1).
3. Thirty-One Floors: Ruta slips, Kostas carries her in, and the secret of his young body comes out; he orders without the coin, and their sofa stays in your walk (Rep +1).
4. The Flat: both ask you to lie for the other one; you tell the truth or pick a lie, and their first loud fight empties two machines (Rep -1).
5. No Coin: they argue for fun and cancel the divorce; Kostas starts carrying old people down the towers (3% more customers, Rep +2), and they leave you the coin.
- Evening: The Coin on Its Edge: the coin lands on its edge in a crack in your bar, so nobody knows who pays.
- Evening: Forty-Six Years of Heads: Ruta's notebook of everything the coin decided, from the dog's name to forty-one summers at a seaside she hates.
