# Valicia — voice sheet

Five lines. Recording format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Who she is

- **How she is:** extreme social anxiety. When she is nervous she talks fast and quietly, apologises before she has done anything wrong, and rehearses conversations before she has them. Talking to people uses up her air: she can hold it for a while, then she has to get away and breathe. She is funny without meaning to be.
- **Her story in five lines:** at the start she backs away from you. At the end she says hi first. Line 4 is where she explains herself; line 5 is pure joy.
- **Not decided yet:** her age, her look, where you meet her, and why she is like this.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
