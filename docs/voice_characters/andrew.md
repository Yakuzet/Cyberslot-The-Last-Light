# Andrew — voice sheet (the story)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

## Who he is

- **What he is:** the hero's best friend, around thirty, part of their crew. He carries the gear, talks too much and complains about everything, and he is funny about it.
- **How he is:** nervous energy, jokes as a shield, loyal (or so it seems). In the tower he dies in front of his friends. Later he comes back, rebuilt with machine parts, and he is not the same man: calm, cold and mocking.
- **His voice:** quick and chatty in the first half; the same voice, slower and colder, after he comes back. Never a cartoon villain: the jokes are still there, they just cut now.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
