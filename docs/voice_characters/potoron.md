# Potoron — voice sheet

Five lines. Recording format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Who he is

- **What he is:** a short, round man who lives inside his VR headset. Cables run from his chest to a humming backpack with a tall antenna. He says he is the best hacker alive. Nobody has checked.
- **Where you meet him:** wandering between the slot machines with his headset on, making noises. He is a comedy character.
- **How he is:** excitable, sweaty, sincere. He panics fast, falls in love fast, and recovers in about ten seconds. He is not a creep, just a man with no filter and no shame.
- **His voice:** "a voice like a kettle": high, breathy, always about to boil over. He jumps from a whisper to a shout inside one sentence.
- **Note for the actor:** the humour is for adults. In his first scene he gets caught in public enjoying something very private in VR. Nothing explicit is shown or said, but there is moaning, and his jokes stay in that territory.
- **What this sheet leaves out:** what he actually hacks, and who comes looking for him.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
