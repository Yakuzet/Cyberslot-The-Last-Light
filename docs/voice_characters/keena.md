# Keena — voice sheet

Five audition lines first, then every line in the game (185, at the end of this sheet). Recording format: see [README.md](README.md).

*Keena is a working name; it may still change.*

## Who she is

- **What she is:** a professional mourner. Rich families hire her to cry at funerals, because a real person crying is the most expensive thing at a service now. She carries a black card with her rates on the back.
- **Where you meet her:** at the bar of the slot shop, wailing at full volume. It is a rehearsal for a funeral at four. When you walk up she stops instantly, her face completely dry.
- **How she is:** precise, theatrical, darkly funny. She rates everything out of ten. She can cry on cue in four seconds, in any style. In her own voice, between performances, she is flat, quick and dry.
- **Her two modes:** the performance (a full, beautiful wail, or a single perfect tear) and her own voice (clipped, unimpressed, very funny). She switches between them with no warm-up at all.
- **What this sheet leaves out:** why she does this, and why she can't cry for real. It comes out slowly over her story. Play her as someone who is the best in the world at something she can no longer feel.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
