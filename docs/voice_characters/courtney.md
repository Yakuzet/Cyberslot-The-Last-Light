# Courtney — voice sheet (the story)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

## Who she is

- **What she is:** the hero's wife, around thirty. She is the clear head of their little crew: she plans, keeps Andrew in line, and gives the orders when the shooting starts.
- **How she is:** sharp, teasing and quick, warm with her husband, tough under fire. She is pregnant during the story, and keeps it to herself until a quiet minute in a lift.
- **Her voice:** a natural, confident voice with a teasing edge. In the gunfight she is loud and clear: short orders. She never sounds like a villain, even in the last scenes, where she is cold and frightened at once.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
