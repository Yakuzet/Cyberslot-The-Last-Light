# Uncle Hüseyin — voice sheet (the story)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

## Who he is

- **What he is:** an old man, in his seventies, who runs a small slot shop in an underground walk. Everybody calls him Uncle. He fixes his old machines with his own hands, and his knuckles are big and crooked.
- **How he is:** warm, nosy and funny in plain words. He pours tea for everyone, adds sugar nobody asked for, and talks about his machines like people. Not a wise old master: an ordinary man the whole street loves.
- **His voice:** warm and a little rough with age, unhurried, with a laugh that turns into a cough. **His name is said HOO-say-in.** Any natural accent is fine; a light Turkish accent would suit him.
- **Language:** English.
