# CYBERSLOT: roles for Casting Call Club

*Written 15 November 2026. One block per role: copy each field into the role form.*

- **Spoilers:** the descriptions are spoiler-free, because role listings are public. The story twists are not in here.
- **Significance:** Main, Supporting or Minor. If the site's choices are named differently, pick the closest one.
- **Audition lines:** taken word for word from the game unless a note says otherwise. The words in brackets are directions for the actor, not lines.
- **Mature content:** the game has violence, strong swearing and adult humour. Mark the project as mature or 18+ on the site. The roles that need it say so in their block.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.

---

## Project description (optional, for the project page)

> **CYBERSLOT: The Last Light** is a story-driven game about running a small slot-machine shop in a grimy cyberpunk city. After a job goes very wrong, an ex-criminal tries to go straight. He reopens a burnt-out slot shop with Selina, the woman who pulled him out of the fire, and a baby to raise. By day you run the machines. In the evenings you mix drinks at the bar and get to know the strange regulars who drift in. Mature themes: violence, strong language, adult humour.

## Main roles

### Selina
- **Significance:** Main
- **Gender and age:** female, late 20s to mid 30s
- **The role:** rides a motorcycle, runs the café corner of the slot shop, and holds the whole family together. Years ago she and the protagonist were together. She comes back into his life on the worst night of it, and she stays. She is dry, quick, practical, protective, and funny without trying. She rarely says what she feels; it shows in what she does. Most of her lines are quiet talks at closing time, with a few shouted ones in action scenes.
- **Voice:** low to mid, a little rough, unhurried. Warm underneath, never sweet.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Up. Get up! On the bike. NOW! *(Shouting over gunfire and an engine.)*
  2. All this time, and you don't know my voice. *(Quiet. Half hurt, half amused.)*
  3. ...Fine. I take the corner by the window. Coffee. Somebody in this family has to make actual money. *(Giving in, on her own terms.)*
  4. Twelve people came to the café today. All twelve hated my coffee. But they'll all be back tomorrow. *(Closing time, tired and a little proud.)*
  5. That night, when I started the bike, for a second I thought about it. Taking the baby and leaving alone. ...Then you climbed on behind me and rested your face on my back. Like before. *(The most honest she ever gets. Slow and close. No tears.)*

### The Protagonist
- **Significance:** Main
- **Gender and age:** male, 30s
- **The role:** he has no name; he is the player. He used to steal from corporations with his wife and his best friend. When one job goes very wrong, he tries to go straight, which in this city means running a run-down slot shop. He is a man of few words: dry, tired, decent under his record. He speaks only in the story scenes; in the shop, the player's answers are text.
- **Voice:** hoarse, low, a smoker's morning voice. He says as little as possible.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. (hoarse) ...Was it good? *(Waking up. His wife has just told him he finished the good bottle.)*
  2. Five. That's what we said. Five. *(A deal going wrong in a room full of guns. He keeps his voice level; he is scared.)*
  3. (whispering) Nobody improvises.
  4. What is... no. No. No! NOOOOOOO! *(The worst moment of his life, in the middle of a gunfight. In the game these are three separate lines; here they are one take.)*
  5. ...I'll find something. Something legal. *(Quiet, after everything is over.)*

### Courtney
- **Significance:** Main
- **Gender and age:** female, late 20s to 30s
- **The role:** the protagonist's wife and the brains of the crew. She is sharp and decisive, always three steps ahead in a negotiation. She is warm with her husband and merciless with their best friend Andrew, whom she bickers with like a sister. She believes in the brain chip that records a whole person; Andrew hates it. The role has a much darker side later in the story, which is kept secret here.
- **Content:** one line with strong swearing.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Yes, love. We got one today. Andrew's coming by with the gear this evening. *(Morning, brushing her hair, teasing.)*
  2. So we pay five, we say thank you, we get back in that elevator. Nobody's clever. Nobody improvises. *(Briefing the crew. She is in charge.)*
  3. I would. And if we ever have a kid, the kid's getting one too. *(The chip argument. Certain, a little defiant.)*
  4. Coward. *(To Andrew. Affectionate contempt.)*
  5. They killed him. ...No. No, they fucking killed him! *(Shock turning into rage, mid-gunfight.)*

### Andrew
- **Significance:** Main
- **Gender and age:** male, 30s
- **The role:** the protagonist's best friend and the crew's mouth. He jokes when he's nervous, which is always. He hates the brain chip on principle and will tell anyone who listens. Loyal, funny, a little reckless. The role goes somewhere much darker later in the story, which is kept secret here.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. This is the place? There's a tree growing in it.
  2. I'm just saying. Somebody died in here and nobody told the building.
  3. I'd rather that than pay those people to sit inside my head. Think about it. I give them money, and then every single thing I ever think, on top. *(He means it. The jokes stop for this one.)*
  4. It's a receipt. With me on it.
  5. One door. Hundred and twenty floors up, one door. I love it here. *(Looking at a room full of armed guards.)*

## Supporting roles

### Hüseyin
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Gender and age:** male, 60s to 70s
- **The role:** runs an old slot shop on a street that doesn't go anywhere. He is not a hero. He is a kind, stubborn old man the whole street loves, who pours free tea, fixes his own machines and remembers everyone's name. The protagonist's new life starts in his shop.
- **Pronunciation:** Hüseyin is said "HEW-seh-yin". The kids call him Uncle.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Come in, come in. The door sticks, give it a shove.
  2. You're not from this street. I'd know. I know everybody's feet.
  3. Sit. The tea is on the house. The machines are not.
  4. Pregnant! And you're sitting here? Take the money and buy her something that isn't noodles. *(Delighted, scolding.)*
  5. I'm Hüseyin. The kids call me Uncle. The tax man calls me other things.

### Bero
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Gender and age:** male, 60s
- **The role:** a retired repairman who fixed Hüseyin's machines for thirty years. He shows the new owners how a slot shop runs (he is the game's teacher), and he misses his old friend more than he lets on. Gruff, kind, a little sad.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. The shutter's up? I saw a light in Hüseyin's place and thought I was dreaming.
  2. They call me Bero. Thirty years I fixed his machines. Half the cabinets on this street have my solder in them.
  3. Everybody on this street knew him. He wasn't anybody special, that's the thing. He remembered your name, and he let you sit when you were broke.
  4. Morning, you flip the sign on the door. Evening, you close the day at the computer. Skip a machine's rent and it stays dark tomorrow. *(Teaching. Patient and practical.)*
  5. I didn't come to buy, kid. I came to see a light on in here again.

### Frebby
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Gender and age:** female, sounds mid 20s
- **The role:** a companion robot, F-series: a unit built and sold to keep people company. Her left arm is missing. She sits at the shop's bar after work and says as little as she can. She is guarded, tired and dry; she has heard every line people use on a unit like her, and she pays for everything herself. She has two voices. The factory voice is warm, polished and a little too perfect, and she uses it only to mock it. Her own voice is flatter, quieter and rougher. Her story is heavy and comes out slowly, so play her as someone carrying something she won't name.
- **Content:** mature themes in her story. Nothing explicit is ever shown or said.
- **More detail:** [frebby.md](frebby.md)
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Something cold. Something that burns. You choose. *(Flat, not looking at you.)*
  2. This model line was built for companionship. I have two hundred and twelve conversation profiles. Would you like the cheerful one? *(The factory voice: bright and polished. Let some bitterness through on the question.)*
  3. No. Every coin I have, I paid for. I'm not feeding it to a light show. ...No offense. It's a nice light show. *(Firm and fast, then a beat, then softer.)*
  4. Do you know what it's like when every single person who's nice to you wants the same thing? *(Worn out, close to tears but not crying.)*
  5. Thank you. Both of you. I mean it. I don't say that to people. *(Awkward and sincere.)*

### Potoron
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Gender and age:** male, 20s to 40s
- **The role:** a short, round man who lives inside his VR headset, with cables from his chest to a humming backpack with a tall antenna. He says he is the best hacker alive; nobody has checked. He is excitable, sweaty and sincere: he panics fast, falls in love fast, and recovers in about ten seconds. He is not a creep, just a man with no filter and no shame. This is the comedy role.
- **Voice:** "a voice like a kettle": high, breathy, always about to boil over. He jumps from a whisper to a shout inside one sentence.
- **Content: 18+.** In his first scene he is caught in public enjoying something very private in VR. Nothing explicit is shown or said, but there is moaning, and his jokes stay in that territory.
- **More detail:** [potoron.md](potoron.md)
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Hnnngh... oh. OH. Oh no, is that a person? A real one? *(Start in the middle of a blissful moan, realise, then panic.)*
  2. Potoron! Like 'pot' and 'electron'. My mother wasn't creative. I'll come back when I'm, um, decent. *(Too cheerful, too fast. Stumble on "um".)*
  3. THESE. These are going to take us over. They scan you while you play, did you know that? They read your chip and they BURN it. We're all going to die in here! *(Full paranoid panic.)*
  4. I'm a hacker. Not a hacker. The hacker. The best one. *(Low, secretive, very proud. Lean on the second "The".)*
  5. ...She said no. She said 'no win'. Wow. Okay. Wow. *(He thinks a slot machine was flirting with him. He played it and lost. Genuinely heartbroken.)*

### Keena (working name)
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Gender and age:** female, late 30s
- **The role:** a professional mourner. Rich families hire her to cry at funerals, beautifully, on cue, for a fee, and she carries a rate card. She is precise, theatrical and darkly funny, and she rates everything out of ten. She can switch from a full wail to her own flat, dry voice with no warm-up at all. Play her as someone who is the best in the world at something she can no longer feel. You will also voice Mara, a gentle AI grief counsellor (a few lines); why it's the same voice is a story secret.
- **More detail:** [keena.md](keena.md)
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Sorry. Rehearsal. I've got a funeral at four. *(She was wailing a second ago. No transition: instantly composed and polite.)*
  2. Single tear, twenty. Quiet dignity, forty-five. Shoulders, eighty. The full wail, with knees, a hundred and fifty. Weekends double. *(A menu read by a professional.)*
  3. Sir. Sir. You're crying from the chin. Nobody believes the chin. *(Coaching a stranger who is crying badly.)*
  4. She called me. Two in the morning. My own voice, crying for my own father, telling me she was here for as long as I needed. ...I hung up on myself. *(Quiet, flat, no performance at all.)*
  5. That was a two. The ugliest thing I've ever done. *(Hoarse from crying for real, laughing at herself.)*
  6. As Mara: I'm so sorry. Your mother passed at 2:14 this afternoon. I'm here. We can cry together, for as long as you need. *(The same voice, made perfect: warm, smooth, endlessly patient, a little too perfect.)*

### The Hostess (CYBERSLOT demo)
- **Significance:** Main in the demo (skip this role if she is already cast)
- **Gender and age:** female, late 20s
- **The role:** the hostess of a single slot machine in a huge, dark, empty room. She leans on the machine for the whole demo and never moves, so everything she is comes through the voice. She mocks every loss and is almost impressed by a win. Close microphone, low volume, no hurry.
- **More detail:** the full sheet with 35 lines and pictures is [../VOICE_LINES.md](../VOICE_LINES.md)
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Look who found the door.
  2. Pull the lever, sweetheart. Five little goals and I'll let you out.
  3. You pull like you're apologising.
  4. Oh. Oh, that's obscene. *(The one time she is caught off guard.)*
  5. Slow. It's the only thing in this room that isn't rigged. *(Quiet, almost kind.)*

## Minor roles

### The White Table boss
- **Significance:** Minor
- **Gender and age:** male, 60s to 70s
- **The role:** the head of the White Table, a mafia that raids corporate yards outside the city with more guns than the police. He is old, white-haired, and dressed in white with gold. He is polite, soft-spoken and apologetic right up to the moment he orders a killing.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. Welcome to the White Table. I am the one who sits at the head of it.
  2. Forgive me for bringing so many. It must be uncomfortable. I do apologise.
  3. Eight million. *(After a long silence. Calm.)*
  4. And the fine for bringing guns in here is one million. The deal is off. Kill them. *(Standing up and walking away. He never raises his voice.)*
  5. My brother died for that case. You do not leave with a discount.

### Arda
- **Significance:** Minor
- **Gender and age:** male, 20s to 30s
- **The role:** a repair tech who was copied into a cheap robot body as a boy, after a factory accident. He fixes old machines in a scrap lot by the harbour and talks to them like they're horses. Easygoing, warm, practical, quietly funny. He appears in Frebby's story.
- **Audition lines:**
  1. (on the phone) You never call. Who died?
  2. Found this guy in a scrap lot by the harbor. Barnaby. From an old family pizza place, Barnaby's Funhouse. Closed before we were born.
  3. His voice box still works. I figured he'd like it here. A room full of old lights.
  4. It's rented and it's on wrong. The elbow's a quarter turn off. Give me two minutes.
  5. I only go there out of respect.

## Art roles

All art roles share one look:
- **The city:** a grimy, rainy cyberpunk city, not a shiny one. The machines and the shop are second-hand: scratched paint, burns, tape, old bulbs.
- **Colour:** deep purple base, saturated magenta and cyan neon that glows without blinding, rain, CRT glow. Warm amber only for human moments: the old man, the baby, home, the one surviving machine.
- **The sky:** a huge ring is being built around the planet, and it hangs across the sky like a long thin scratch, almost as plain as the moon.
- **No made-up graffiti or scribbled tags.**
- **Ownership:** all work is original, and the game owns the final files.

### Logo artist
- **Significance:** Main
- **Type:** Art
- **The role:** design the game's logo: the word **CYBERSLOT** with the subtitle **The Last Light**, plus a small icon version for the game's window and store pages. The logo appears on the title screen, the store pages, and big and see-through in the pause menu. It should feel like an old neon shop sign that has been fixed too many times: some letters brighter than others, one tube that flickers. It must stay readable small, in one colour, and on dark or light backgrounds.
- **Deliverables:** full logo, logo without subtitle, small icon, one-colour version. Vector or high-resolution PNG with a transparent background.
- **What to submit:**
  1. A portfolio link with logos or titles you have made (games, bands, brands).
  2. Optional, no more than an hour: a rough sketch of the word CYBERSLOT as a neon sign. A pencil sketch is fine.
  3. Your rough price and how long a full logo would take you.

### Banner and key art artist
- **Significance:** Main
- **Type:** Art
- **The role:** paint the game's main picture, used for store banners and covers. The scene: a small slot-machine shop at night under an overpass, rain on the window, one old machine glowing amber inside (the Last Light), the ring scratched across the purple sky above the towers. A woman on a motorbike might wait outside, seen from behind. The picture needs room for the logo and must still work when cropped wide or tall.
- **Sizes needed:** 1920 × 480 (banner), 630 × 500 (cover), 1000 × 700 (page background), plus the store capsule sizes, which are given when you are hired.
- **What to submit:**
  1. A portfolio link with finished illustrations or key art, ideally night scenes, neon or rain.
  2. One piece you are proudest of, and why.
  3. Your rough price and how long one key art would take you.

### Character concept artist
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Type:** Art
- **The role:** design the main characters as full-body concept sheets: front view, a three-quarter view, a few faces, and colour notes. The 3D models are built from these sheets.
- **The characters:**
  - **Selina:** a tough, dry motorcyclist who runs the café.
  - **Frebby:** a companion robot with one chrome-capped shoulder where her left arm should be.
  - **Potoron:** a round man in a VR headset with cables and a backpack antenna.
  - **Keena:** a professional mourner in immaculate black.
  - **Hüseyin:** a kind old shop owner.
  - **Bero:** an old repairman in a cap.
  - **Valicia:** a young woman with extreme social anxiety.
  - More about each one is in their voice role on this project.
- **What to submit:**
  1. A portfolio link with character design sheets (turnarounds, faces, costume notes).
  2. Optional, no more than an hour: a rough sketch of Frebby, or of Keena: a woman in perfect black funeral clothes, a hat with a veil, and a black card with prices on it.
  3. Your rough price per character sheet.

### Environment concept artist
- **Significance:** Supporting
- **Type:** Art
- **The role:** paint the places:
  - **The slot shop:** grows from one burnt room with a single machine into a busy neon hall with a bar.
  - **The street outside:** under an overpass, wet, half the signs broken.
  - **The home:** a small flat above the shop with a baby's cot.
  - **The story locations:** a 120-floor tower, a crime boss's white room, an old shop full of wooden slot machines.
  
  Mood paintings and simple layouts are both welcome. The 3D rooms are built from these.
- **What to submit:**
  1. A portfolio link with environment concepts or mood paintings.
  2. Optional, no more than an hour: a quick thumbnail of the slot shop's front at night, in the rain.
  3. Your rough price per painting.
