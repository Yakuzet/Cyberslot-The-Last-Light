# Bero — voice sheet (the shop)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

## Who he is

- **What he is:** a retired repairman, around sixty. For thirty years he fixed the machines of Hüseyin, the old man who ran this shop before the fire. Hüseyin is dead now, and Bero misses him.
- **What he does:** he teaches the player how to run the shop. His lessons are the game's real rules, so they need to be clear.
- **How he is:** gruff, kind, practical; he talks about machines more easily than about feelings. The sadness comes out in one or two short lines.
- **His voice:** an older working man's voice, warm and a little rough, unhurried. **His name is said BEH-ro.**
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
