# Frebby — voice sheet

Five lines. Recording format: see [README.md](README.md).

## Who she is

- **What she is:** a companion robot, F-series: a unit built and sold to keep people company. She looks like a woman in her mid-twenties. Her left arm is missing. Where it should be there is a scuffed chrome cap and a few loose cables.
- **Where you meet her:** at the bar of the slot shop, after her shift, with rain outside. She orders a drink and says as little as she can.
- **How she is:** guarded, tired, dry. She has heard every line people use on a unit like her and expects the worst. She pays for everything herself and is proud of it.
- **Her two voices:** the voice she was sold with is warm, polished and a little too perfect, like a voice assistant. Her own voice is underneath it: flatter, quieter, rougher. Most of the time you hear her own voice. She switches to the factory one only to mock it.
- **What this sheet leaves out:** where she came from. It comes out slowly over her story, and it is heavy. Play her as someone carrying something she won't name.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
