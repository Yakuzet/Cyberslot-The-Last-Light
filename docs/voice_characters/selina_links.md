# Selina — voice sheet: the social links

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). For who she is and her voice, see [selina_story.md](selina_story.md). These are her lines in the social links: the regulars who visit the shop. She is behind the bar or the café counter, working while she listens: dry, warm, quick, often a one-line answer that cuts in. Every line is copied word for word from the game; words in brackets are stage directions (in the Direction column). Lines are in game order; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.
