# Ruta and Kostas — voice sheet

Two actors: five audition lines each first, then every line in the game (215, at the end of this sheet). Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. *Not in the game yet.*

## Who they are

- **Together:** a couple married forty-six years who have never made a decision without flipping a coin: who talks, who orders, who pays. Now they are getting divorced, one item every Friday, at the slot shop, with the player as their witness. They finish each other's sentences, and they have never had a loud argument in their lives, until they do.
- **Ruta** (ROO-tah): in her seventies, a retired tailor. Small, precise and dry. She reads the rules of their divorce off a card. Her hip is bad, and she hates needing help more than she hates anything.
- **Kostas** (KOS-tahss): seventy-four, a retired furniture carrier. Cheerful, proud, a little slow to sit down. He now lives in a young man's body, a second body he bought on a payment plan. **So his voice is a young man's voice that talks like a grandfather:** an old man's rhythm and old-fashioned manners, coming out of a body of twenty-six.
- **Their voices:** never cartoon "old people" voices. Warm, quick, real. Most of what they say is dry and funny; the big lines are in one fight, where they shout for the first time in their marriage.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as each of you keeps the same one in every line. The dog's name is **Kofte** (KOF-teh).
