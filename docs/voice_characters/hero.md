# The hero — voice sheet (the story)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

## Who he is

- **What he is:** the man the player plays. A former heist man in his thirties: he and his wife Courtney, with their best friend Andrew, did dirty jobs for money. After the night in the tower, he tries to live straight.
- **How he is:** tired, dry and short with words. He jokes when he is scared and goes quiet when he is hurt. He loves Courtney, and Andrew is like a brother to him.
- **His voice:** low and natural, a little rough. Never a movie-hero voice. Most of his lines are short; the big moments are three cries when his best friend dies and one shout in the fire.
- **The player** often answers by picking a reply on screen; those replies are not recorded. Only the lines below are spoken.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
