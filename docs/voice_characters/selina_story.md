# Selina — voice sheet: the story

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here.

## Who she is

- **What she is:** a young woman on a motorbike, around thirty. She knew the hero long before his wife Courtney: she was his girlfriend when he was twenty. They split up years ago, and her feelings never quite ended.
- **What she does in the story:** on the worst night of the hero's life, she rides her motorbike through a shop window, pulls him out of a fire, and takes a baby with her. Afterwards she stays. She helps him raise the baby (Ada) and open a slot shop, where she runs the little café.
- **How she is:** dry, quick and practical. She says less than she feels. She teases instead of comforting, and her tenderness shows only in small moments: a hand on a shoulder, a joke at the right time. She has been strong alone for a long time.
- **Her voice:** low, warm and a little rough, with a tired humour. Never cute, never girlish, never a "tough girl" growl either. She rarely raises her voice: the two lines in the fire are the only time she shouts.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
- **The hero** never speaks out loud in her evening talks: the player answers by picking a reply. So in those scenes she is reacting to him, and some lines answer different replies. Each line is recorded once.

## 1. The fire

She has just ridden her bike through the shop window into a burning room full of smoke, shot the man who was about to kill the hero, and grabbed the baby. Seconds count.

| File | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `selina_fire_1.wav` | Up. Get up! | Shouted over fire and broken glass, straight at him on the floor. Urgent, not panicked |
| `selina_fire_2.wav` | On the bike. NOW! | A command. Louder. The last word hits hard |

## 4. Her first evening talk: "The good bottle"

She has poured two glasses of a bad drink and asks the hero to tell her what happened to him, from the start. He tells her; she listens, asks, remembers their old days on her bike. About five minutes in the game.
