# Selina — voice sheet: the shop

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. For who she is and her voice, see [selina_story.md](selina_story.md).

**Where she is:** in the shop game, Selina runs the little café inside the slot shop she owns with the hero, and helps raise the baby, Ada. These are her everyday lines: in the morning, while he plays the machines, at closing time, and at night upstairs.

**How to play them:** relaxed, warm, dry. She is tired and busy but she likes this life. Short lines, quick, no big acting.

**What to record first:** section 1. Players hear these the most. The other sections can follow.
