# The White Table — voice sheet (the story)

Recording format: see [README.md](README.md). Every line is copied word for word from the game. Words in brackets in the game are stage directions; they are not spoken, so they are in the "Direction" column here. Lines are listed in the order they come in the game; a talk with choices can list lines from different answers, so record each line once.

Two roles from the crime gang in the tower: their boss, and a guard with one line (at the end of this sheet).

## The old man in white (the boss)

- **What he is:** the head of the White Table, a crime gang that robs company convoys. An old man with white hair, a white suit and thin gold lines at the cuffs.
- **How he is:** polite, soft-spoken and patient: he apologises for bringing so many armed men. That politeness is what makes him frightening. When he says "Kill them", he says it like the end of a meeting.
- **His voice:** old, calm, precise. He never shouts, even when he taunts them over a loudspeaker from his machine on the roof.
- **Language and accent:** English. Any natural accent is fine as long as it stays the same in every line.
